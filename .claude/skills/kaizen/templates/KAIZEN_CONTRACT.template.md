## What happens on failed tests

A failed experiment is never called a win. It is reverted when safe, the
lesson is recorded, and a revised idea goes to the backlog only if justified.

## What happens on critical findings

Critical security, data-loss, payment, or reliability findings outrank the
stated goal and are brought to you first.

## What happens when user action is required

I stop, explain simply, and wait for your okay.

## Pause / stop

`/kaizen pause` disables future scheduling but keeps all Memory.
`/kaizen stop` stops scheduling without deleting Memory. Deleting Memory is
always a separate explicit action.
